questions. answered.

can i try just one envelope first?
yes — "try one envelope" gets you a single one-off envelope for $18. no subscription, no commitment. if you love it, subscribe from the next month. and if it makes you feel nothing, you're covered by the smile-or-cringe refund.
when does my first envelope ship?
sign up by the 15th of the month and your envelope ships at the end of that month. after the 15th, you're in the following month's send. delivery takes 2–3 weeks to Europe and 4–5 weeks to the USA, Australia and the rest of the world.
why does it take so long?
because it's real. it's made by hand and crosses the planet from a small post office in Hội An. the wait is part of it — you'll get a message from me on shipping day so you know it's on its way.
is there tracking?
no tracking — it keeps costs down, and honestly, waiting without knowing is more fun. you'll get a message from me on shipping day.
what if it doesn't arrive?
if your envelope hasn't arrived after 6 weeks, email me and i'll resend. it's rare, but it happens, and i'll always make it right.
can i cancel?
yes, anytime. just email floatingboobs69@gmail.com before the 15th and i'll sort it out before the next send. no awkward emails, no guilt trips.
what's the mystery envelope?
a tiny sealed envelope inside the main one — but it's not for you. instructions inside tell you what to do with it. give it to someone. leave it somewhere. pass it on. don't open it yourself. that's the whole point.
is the art different every month?
yes. new print, new postcard, new sticker, new fortune strip, every single month. no repeats.
